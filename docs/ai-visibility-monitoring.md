## How to Check

### ChatGPT
1. Go to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com)
2. Search your target page URL or key phrases
3. Note if your content is cited and how

### Perplexity
1. Go to [perplexity.ai](https://perplexity.ai)
2. Search your target page URL or key phrases
3. Note citations and source attribution

### Google AI Overviews
1. Search in Google with your target query
2. Look for AI Overview section at top
3. Note if your content appears

### Search Console
1. Open Google Search Console
2. Check Performance report for AI Overview impressions
3. Monitor CTR changes

### GA4
1. Open GA4 dashboard
2. Track traffic from AI-driven sources
3. Monitor engagement metrics for AI-referred visitors

---

## What to Track Over Time

- **Citation frequency** — How often your content appears in AI responses
- **Citation context** — Is your content used as a primary source or secondary?
- **Traffic attribution** — Visits from AI sources (ChatGPT, Perplexity, etc.)
- **Engagement metrics** — Bounce rate, time on page, conversions from AI referrals
- **Query coverage** — Which of your priority queries show your content
- **Competitive position** — Are you cited over competitors?
